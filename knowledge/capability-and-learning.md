# Capability and learning decision guide

## Capability data model

A capability is a governed skill record. Job profiles link a department, normalized title, job level, and required skills. Course coverage links an active course to one or more skills and a supported proficiency level. Employee evidence comes from completed mapped assignments; assignment alone is not evidence of proficiency.

The source and review date of each requirement matter. Role requirements should be reviewed by the business owner and People or Learning team. Course mappings should be reviewed when course content changes. Retired courses and inactive skills are excluded from new recommendations.

## Prioritizing internal capability gaps

Prioritize a capability when a required role has active employees without completed mapped learning, matching open requisitions, a mandatory control requirement, or a documented workforce plan. The recommendation should state the affected job profile, skill, eligible employee count, completed evidence count, matching course, and open-role context.

High priority is appropriate for a governed control requirement or a broad gap combined with active demand. Medium priority is appropriate for a smaller development gap or watch condition. A recommendation with no active course mapping is a curriculum gap, not an assignment recommendation.

## External market relevance

The current workspace can describe internal demand but cannot claim what the external market requires without an approved external source. Market relevance needs a governed skills feed containing occupation or role mapping, skill name, demand signal, geography, observation date, source license, and refresh cadence.

When an external feed is absent, say that the recommendation is based on internal job profiles, hiring demand, and completed learning evidence. Do not invent market trends. When a feed is added, keep external demand separate from internal requirements so HR can see why a recommendation exists.

## Selecting a learning intervention

Confirm that the gap is teachable, the course covers the required skill, the employee has access and time, and the course matches their role and development goals. Compare a course with mentoring, project experience, documentation, pairing, certification, or internal mobility. Training is not the default response to compensation, workload, manager-support, or role-clarity evidence.

For a cohort, preview exact recipients and duplicate suppressions before confirmation. Use one campaign for common rationale and due date, with individual assignments for completion evidence. Managers should see their team assignments; employees should see and complete their own assignments in the employee portal.

## Measuring learning outcomes

Track assignment reach, completion, overdue status, assessment evidence where appropriate, employee feedback, and later application of the skill. Completion rate alone does not prove proficiency or business impact. Do not treat an assessment score as a performance rating unless the organization has an explicit validated policy.

For capability coverage, report the active role population, employees with evidence, employees needing evidence, and the data freshness date. Review mappings quarterly and retire recommendations that no longer match the workforce plan.
