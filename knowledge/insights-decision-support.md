# Workforce insights and decision support

## Decision hierarchy

Start with operating exceptions, then show the evidence needed to act. Current headcount and open work are snapshots. Hires, exits, leave, completed learning, and promotions are dated events. Every rate must use a denominator from the same department, location, and employee-status scope.

An executive summary should answer: what changed, where pressure is concentrated, what is already covered, what remains unowned, and what HR should review next. Raw chart volume is not insight.

## Department operating view

Compare active headcount, recorded attrition rate, company-rate delta, vacancy rate, completed hires, exits, net movement, open requisitions, and replacement coverage. Counts without rates can mislead when departments differ in size. A coverage gap is a staffing-planning signal; it is not proof of delivery failure.

## Talent supply

Use completed hires, average time to hire, current open requisitions, candidate stage, offer follow-up, hiring-source volume, and role refill history. Hiring-source speed and volume do not establish quality of hire. A role continuity review should combine active incumbents, recorded exits, completed hires, open roles, and refill time.

## Replacement scenario

Replacement cost is a scenario calculated from recorded annual pay coverage and explicit assumptions for recruiting, vacancy productivity, refill days, and onboarding ramp. It is not an accounting actual, guaranteed loss, or expected saving. Report pay coverage and the refill basis. If pay is missing, do not silently treat it as zero; state which cost components remain available.

Do not multiply a model probability by replacement cost and present the result as expected financial exposure. Model review evidence and replacement economics answer different questions.

## Capability and mobility

Learning completion, mandatory gaps, role requirements, mapped course evidence, open requisitions, and promotion history support capability planning. A course is appropriate only when a confirmed skill requirement and employee access exist. Course cost versus a replacement scenario is a planning comparison, not evidence that training will prevent attrition.

Mobility review identifies employees with tenure and incomplete promotion history for human review. It does not decide who should be promoted.

## Durable action queue

An analytics exception becomes operational only after a work item is created with an owner, due date, next action, and completion evidence. The action remains open until the domain service records the outcome. Navigation to Onboarding, Learning, Leaves, People, or Work queue must preserve a stable record identifier or validated filter.
